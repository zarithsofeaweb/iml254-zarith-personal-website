K-Pop Culture

Favorite Group: IVE
My Ultimate Bias: Jang Wonyoung
I fell in love with K-pop when I was having a hard time in my life and it helps me forget about the reality. I enjoy listening to music, watching performances and learning about the artist journey and hard work behind their success. My favourite Kpop group is IVE. IVE songs are catchy and enjoyable to listen to which is one of the reason why I become a fan of the group. Beside their music, the members positive personalities are also reasons why I love them. Jang Wonyoung is my favourite member because of her mindset and they way she brings herself so confident. That always makes me adore her more. The way she tries to stay positive even during her difficult situations teaches me to push myself harder and see the bright side of the world. Supporting IVE has become one of the major happiness and motivation in my daily life.

My All-Time Favorite Drama

Drama Title: The Untamed
Key Actors Involved: Wang Yibo, Xiao Zhan
The story follows Wei Wuxian (Xiao Zhan) and Lan Wangji (Wang Yibo), two talented disciples of respected clans, meet during cultivation training and accidentally discover a secret carefully hidden for many years. Taking on the legacy of their ancestors, they decide to rid the world of the ominous threat. But in a dramatic turn of events, Wei Wuxian dies. Sixteen years later, Wei Wu Xian is brought back to life through a self-sacrificing ritual. He conceals himself behind a mask and assumes the identity of his summoner. Soon, Wei Wuxian reunites with Lan WangJi and they start working together to solve the mysteries of the present and unravel the truth behind the events in the past.
